Standard Treatment Cost Guidelines

Routine diagnostic blood tests should not exceed the regional standard rate by more than 20%. Charges significantly above this range should be flagged for review.

Length-of-stay guidelines: routine appendectomy recovery is typically 2-3 days. Claims showing significantly longer hospital stays without a documented complication should be flagged for review.

Standard room rent is capped at the amount specified in the policy schedule; charges for a room category above what the policy covers are payable only up to the policy's room rent limit, with associated charges (like doctor visit fees) proportionately adjusted.
