Health Insurance Policy - General Terms

Coverage: This policy covers hospitalization expenses, including room rent, doctor's fees, and diagnostic tests, up to the sum insured stated in the policy schedule.

Waiting Period: Pre-existing conditions are covered only after a waiting period of 24 months from the policy start date. Claims filed for pre-existing conditions within this period will not be covered.

Exclusions: This policy does not cover cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (unless due to an accident), or self-inflicted injuries. Claims arising from participation in adventure sports are excluded unless an adventure sports rider was purchased.

Motor Insurance Policy - General Terms

Coverage: Comprehensive motor policies cover damage from accidents, theft, fire, and natural disasters, subject to the deductible stated in the policy.

Exclusions: Damage caused while driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs is not covered. Claims for mechanical breakdown or normal wear and tear are excluded.
